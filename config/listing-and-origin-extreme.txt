Post-analysis diagnostics requested after primary results and a methods consultation. These diagnostics do not change original or public panel samples, fit models, or designate a new primary estimand.

Listing-date audit: match supplied original stock codes to the existing public panel stock-code/listing-date reference. Check uniqueness and date consistency within code. For each original eligible pair, label origin years strictly less than listing year as pre-listing; years equal to listing year are eligible by calendar year, with within-year reporting availability unresolved. Count no-match or missing-listing-date codes/rows separately. Inspect the original ResearchSample workbook sheet or its previously extracted cached cells for any listing-date field. Do not infer listing status merely from nonmissing source ROE. Audit annual and pooled main2015-2019 and2020 coverage.

Ex-ante origin-extreme diagnostic: read each source's saved predictions and its own saved linear-ROE fit record. For target year y, use the training-only observed current-ROE 1st/99th percentile clipping bounds saved in that fold. An observation is outside when its raw origin ROE is strictly less than the lower bound or strictly greater than the upper bound; equality is inside. No test outcomes enter this classification. Report separately for each source and for main2015-2019 and2020: observations, security count, sample share; MAE for LAD linear, LAD sign-asymmetric, LAD plus accounting signals; each stratum's sum of squared errors and fraction of the total SSE for each model; RMSE and absolute-error share as descriptive complements. Public-panel and original-source diagnostic strata are based on their own ROE definitions and training distributions. No refitting and no thresholds selected after inspecting group outcomes.
