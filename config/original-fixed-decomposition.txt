POST-ANALYSIS fixed-forecast decomposition. This diagnostic was specified after observing primary and earlier sensitivity results. It does not change any model, transformation, tuning parameter, sample filter or primary prediction.

Use pooled 2015–2019 original-source predictions from the four LAD specifications. Determine provenance using only the deposited public listing reference: matched valid date with origin year at/after listing year; matched date with origin before listing year; or unmatched/missing date. The reference has unique codes and valid dates. Cross these three categories with raw origin ROE <0 versus >=0. Report the overall sample, each provenance category, both sign groups and all six crosscells; retain empty cells with undefined metrics and intervals.

For each group report observations, distinct security codes, negative-origin share, four MAEs, each model's share of full-sample absolute error, and four ordering contrasts. The pooled-gain contribution equals group observation share times its paired MAE gain. Verify that observation-weighted group MAEs and gains reconstruct the pooled estimates. Security counts need not add across groups because a security can change groups over time.

For within-group contrast intervals, resample security histories within that group with replacement, jointly over the four model losses and observation count: 2,000 draws, seed 20261025, percentile 95% intervals. Predictions remain fixed. These are within-group intervals; weighted pooled contributions are point decompositions and do not have a cross-group bootstrap interval in this diagnostic.

Join original raw ROE and consolidated net profit by exact security/origin-year and security/target-year keys. Validate uniqueness and ROE scaling. Report negative/zero/positive/missing/nonfinite crosscounts for both periods, distinguishing opposite signs from zero mismatches and missing values. Include an all-source jointly-finite sign sanity check. Do not infer equity validity, insolvency, legal status or reasons for missingness.

For each group count distinct securities absent from the raw 2020 export. This measures export coverage only; it is not a survival or delisting classification. For all main origins and negative-ROE origins, report observed net-profit transitions from negative origin profit to positive, zero or negative next-year profit, including available and missing denominators. Recovery fractions are descriptive and conditional on retained observations.

Read existing listing-screened LAD metrics and linear-to-asymmetric intervals without fitting another model. Keep fixed-full-sample subgroup losses distinct from losses of the separately refitted screened-cohort models. A full-versus-screened refit difference does not identify the removed group's forecasting performance.
